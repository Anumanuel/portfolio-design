02chapter two

A little about me

I'm Anush — a designer who codes (or a developer who can't stop designing, depending on the day). I care about the small decisions: the spacing that lets a page breathe, the micro-interaction that makes someone smile, the label that finally makes sense.

My path started with doodles in the margins of school notebooks, wandered through computer science and found its home in UI/UX. Today I design and build websites for brands, start-ups and curious people — from the very first wireframe to the final deploy.

My design philosophy

“Good design is a conversation, not a decoration.”
  1. Start with people. Pixels come second.
  2. Sketch first. Ugly ideas are welcome on paper.
  3. Sweat the details nobody notices — they'll feel them.

A

Design

making it look & feel right

  • Components, auto-layout & variables — where every idea gets its first pixels.
  • Photo retouching, mood boards & texture work.
  • Icons, illustrations & logos with crisp vectors.
  • User flows, journeys & interfaces that simply make sense.
  • Paper first, then low-fi frames. Always.
  • Clickable flows to test ideas before a single line of code.
  • Tokens, components & docs that keep teams consistent.
B

Development

turning pixels into real things

  • Semantic, accessible markup — the foundation of everything.
  • Grid, Flexbox, custom properties & a lot of love.
  • Mixins & partials for tidy, scalable styles.
  • Vanilla JS for interactions, DOM magic & APIs (this site included!).
  • Maintaining & extending existing projects.
  • Rapid, reliable layouts when deadlines are tight.
  • Utility-first styling for fast, consistent UI.
C

Creative Dev

making things move

  • Timelines & easing for buttery-smooth animation.
  • Experimenting with 3D scenes in the browser.
  • Scroll-driven storytelling & reveals.
  • Micro-interactions that make interfaces feel alive.
  • Layouts that work from a 320px phone to a 4K screen.

currently learning: WebGL shaders & motion design 🌀
